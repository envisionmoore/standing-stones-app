Standing Stones Privacy Policy

Effective October 31, 2026

Standing Stones is a Bible promises app with a prayer journal. It is made by Jeremy Moore. This policy explains what happens to the information you put into the app.

The short version

Everything you write in Standing Stones stays on your phone. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. We never see your prayers. The only information that leaves your phone is the record of a subscription, if you buy one.

What the app keeps, and where

The app stores these things only on your device:

  • your journal entries (prayers), their status and any reminders you set;
  • your altars (answered prayers) and the dates you give them;
  • the promises you save and the folders you put them in;
  • your settings, such as light or dark mode, your Bible translation and the situations shown on Today.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. We have no server that receives it.

Subscriptions

Journal & Altars is an optional subscription. Payment is handled entirely by Apple: we never see your name, Apple Account or payment details.

To know whether you're subscribed, the app uses RevenueCat, a service that checks purchases with Apple. RevenueCat receives a random ID created by the app (not linked to your name or Apple Account), the details of your purchase (which plan, when it started and renews, and the price and country), and basic technical information such as your device type, iOS version and app version. It never receives your prayers, altars or anything else you write. RevenueCat's own privacy policy is at revenuecat.com/privacy.

If you don't subscribe, the app still contacts RevenueCat to show the current prices, but no purchase is recorded.

Things you choose to do

  • Backups. If you tap Export backup in Settings, the app makes a file of your prayers, altars and saved promises, and the phone asks where to put it (for example Files, iCloud Drive or email). Where it goes is up to you. The file is not encrypted, so keep it somewhere private.
  • Sharing. When you share a promise, as text or as a picture, it goes through your phone's share sheet to the app and people you pick. Only the promise is shared, never your prayers.
  • Your phone's own backups. Like other apps, the app's data can be included in your iPhone's iCloud or computer backups, under Apple's terms.

Notifications

Daily promise and prayer reminders are scheduled on your phone itself. They are not sent through any server. If you turn on the prayer lock, reminders never show the words of your prayers.

Face ID and passcode

If you lock your prayers, your iPhone checks your Face ID, Touch ID or passcode. The app only learns whether the check succeeded. It never receives your face, fingerprint or passcode.

Links to outside help

The crisis support card can open your phone or messages app to call or text a helpline, or open findahelpline.com in your browser. Those services have their own privacy policies.

Children

Standing Stones doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children. A subscription record contains no name, email or other personal details.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
