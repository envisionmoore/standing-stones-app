Standing Stones

Bible promises for what you’re facing

Standing Stones gathers God’s promises by the situations people go through, with a Promise of the Day, a prayer journal and altars to remember answered prayer. It’s made by Jeremy Moore.

Support

Questions, problems or ideas: email standingstonesapp@gmail.com. I read every message.

Subscriptions

Journal & Altars is an optional subscription. To cancel or change it, open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions. To restore a purchase on a new phone, open Standing Stones, tap the gear for Settings, and tap Restore purchases.

Your prayers

Everything you write stays on your phone. There are no accounts, so I can’t see or recover your prayers. Use Export backup in the app’s Settings to keep a copy.
